About Our dance studio

Qala by Krishna is a dance and fitness studio in Shastri Nagar, Kanpur. We welcome beginners and experienced dancers to learn, move, and grow through dance.

Our welcoming classes help you build confidence, improve your movement, and enjoy dance in a supportive studio community.
